What is Making Tax Digital (MTD)? Making Tax Digital (MTD) is HMRC’s new initiative to digitise the UK tax system. It requires businesses, self-employed individuals and landlords to keep digital records and submit tax information to HMRC quarterly, using approved software (like Taxfix).
